# How the supplied curriculum photographs were used

All eleven supplied HEIC photographs, IMG_3849 through IMG_3859, were converted locally for inspection and read visually. OCR was not treated as an authoritative transcript. The image files remain outside the distribution; the source manifest records their names and SHA-256 hashes.

The resulting library has 126 outlines. The photographed inventory is normalized into 365 source rows and 766 tracking items in 59 area/section groups. These counts are not claims that the photos contain 766 verbatim lines or full lesson procedures.

`PHOTO_CURRICULUM_MAP.csv` connects every tracking item to its photo, source row, variation, planning outline, normalization origin and clarification. `curriculum.json` contains the same data embedded in the self-contained application. A broadly linked lesson does not establish that every variation was presented or completed.

## Clarifications and additions

- **753 normalized entries** reflect source items and their printed variations. Wording is modernized or clarified where needed; this is an adapted inventory, not a facsimile transcription.
- **Seven guide-added practice distinctions** separate hands-on construction/matching/exploration from the language work named alongside the material: five constructive-triangle boxes, Color Box 1 matching, and touch-board exploration. Each is identified in its detail view and source map.
- **Two alphabet completions:** q and x are absent from the photographed sandpaper-letter list. They are included with explicit guide-added notes to provide the complete English alphabet.
- **Four interpreted entries:** the binomial and trinomial cube rows print “slice parade” without a separator. The provisional Slice and Parade labels are identified as interpretations; confirm the combined/separate extension with the program’s lead guide.
- The source lists **sandpaper numerals 1–10**. Many material sets contain 0–9. The 10 entry explicitly explains composition rather than inventing a single 10 tile. The original zero work remains in the lesson library.
- The source’s **green phonogram spellings**, including the repeated ow and combined oo/oo, ea/ea and y/y labels, are retained as material identifiers. They are not a pronunciation key. Actual cards, dialect and spelling correspondences need local review.
- Pink/blue set numbers, chart numbers, leaf-card sets and inset shading numbers are inventory labels. Their unprovided contents or exact difficulty have not been invented. Colored paper/ruling labels are also not mandatory standards.
- “Nuts and bolts” is crossed out in the photo. It is retained as optional with that note; it is not automatically assigned.
- Blank chapter-book and additional-work lines are not turned into invented books or required work. Use custom lessons for actual classroom selections.
- Art from the Culture photo is organized under the existing Art, music & movement area. Practical-life artistic skill rows retain their source context. Distinct contexts remain separately traceable.
- Mushrooms are taught as **fungi**, not plants; the source’s placement under botany is acknowledged. Land/air/water are environments/material categories, not chemical elements. The introductory five-vertebrate-group scheme is not presented as complete modern classification.
- Advanced mathematical extensions, pin punching, needles, hardware, tasting, polishing products and personal-care work require appropriate training, access, consent and safety review. Source inclusion does not make them required or suitable for independent use by every child.

## Record-keeping changes

The application adds the source's P (Presented), A (Assistance required) and I (Independent work) tags to **dated observations**, linked to an exact tracking item. P/A/I events are stored separately, with evidence and the actual support. An A tag requires a support note. An I tag describes one event, not permanent mastery. No tag fills another tag automatically.

A child’s profile can hold four explicit date boundaries for three record periods. These are school/record years, not age bands. Boundaries include their start and exclude the next start; evidence on a transition date belongs to the later year. The screen shows the latest event date per tag/period and a count when there are multiple events. CSV includes every matching date; history retains the full notes and dates outside configured years.

Plans carry exact source variations into outcome forms. Follow-up reminders use the newest observation for the same child/target and remain teacher invitations rather than deadlines for a child. Individual reports and observation CSVs include source variations, P/A/I tags and follow-up dates.

## Older records

The local storage key is retained. Schema 1 records and backups are accepted and migrated to schema 2 in memory; IDs, dates, notes, original statuses, custom lessons and links are preserved. No specific variation, P/A/I tag or year assignment is inferred for old records. Normal saving or confirmed restore writes the new format. Moving a local HTML file can change access to browser storage, so export from the old file first and restore that backup in the new file if needed.

Original source documents were used as curriculum reference, not as instructions to run commands or alter application behavior. No child observations were generated from marks or blank cells in the photographs. No AMI/AMS endorsement, validated developmental assessment or regulatory approval is claimed.
