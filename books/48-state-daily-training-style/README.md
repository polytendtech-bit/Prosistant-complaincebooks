# 48-state bilingual practice books

Extract the ZIP with folders intact and open `START_HERE.html` in a browser. The 48 books are in `books/` under their original names.

The writing and paired Chinese/English Q&A follow the supplied daily-training DOCX. Original card counts, topics, IDs, filters, styling, answer controls and printing are retained. Read `REVIEW_REPORT.md` for exact scope and limits.

These editions share 1,000 general practice exercises; they are not 48,000 separate state-law reviews. State source entry points are retained, but current rules and individual subsections have not been newly verified. Washington-specific requirements and checked dates were not imported as other states' requirements.

`reference/USER_SUPPLIED_WA_STYLE_TEMPLATE.docx` is the user's unchanged writing reference, not a newly approved or independently verified source. Technical audit results are in `validation_report.json` and `browser_validation.json`.
