# 48-state books — Washington daily-training writing style

## Delivered scope

All 48 uploaded state books have been rewritten using the supplied Washington daily compliance training DOCX as the writing and bilingual question/answer reference. California and Washington books were not part of this 48-book rewrite and were not overwritten.

Each uploaded book was inspected directly. Each contains 1,000 exercises, 25 topics and 500 center / 500 family-home practice entries. Their shared baseline content and state-specific source routes matched the retained earlier edition. The revised books keep those counts, case/core/family IDs, topic positions and setting attributes.

The uploaded DOCX matches the previously supplied reference document by SHA-256. Its historical rule-check statements remain source-document claims; they are not new verification for these state editions.

## Writing and Q&A changes

The reference document describes an actual incident, includes a proposed shortcut or unresolved fact, asks the director to make decisions, and separates the answer into practical categories. This edition applies that pattern:

1. A distinct bilingual incident focus, such as a conflicting authorization, a handoff gap, an incomplete record or an unsafe proposed shortcut.
2. A staff/family decision context with competing proposals, rather than a short abstract statement alone.
3. Paired Chinese and English questions addressed to the director or family-home provider.
4. **必须事项（初稿） / Required actions (draft)** — introductory safeguards and truthful record handling.
5. **实务处理 / Practical approach** — the reason for the particular concern and the appropriate decision task.
6. **记录与最佳实践 / Records and best practice** — actual observations, actions, responsibility and results, with uncertainty preserved.
7. **待确认事项 / Items to verify** — license, age, role, program, exceptions and reporting questions that still require the applicable text.
8. **官方资料查阅入口 / Official source route** — the original uploaded state's own licensing entry point, clearly labeled as a research route rather than a verified case citation.

The shared curriculum has 1,000 different full English scenario texts and 1,000 different full Chinese scenario texts. It retains the existing 100-family / ten-decision-task organization. Related cases can still practice the same underlying principle with different facts. Exact-text uniqueness does not establish 1,000 distinct statutory requirements.

Chinese incident/question text appears before its paired English text, matching the DOCX presentation. Questions come before the expandable answer; the former answer-revealing teaching note is removed from that position. The answer divisions follow the DOCX, while the location and behavior of the answer panel remain those of the original HTML reader. No new personal-data form or response storage has been added.

## State-law and template limits

The 48 editions share general child-care practice material. They are not 48,000 independently authored or individually reviewed state-law cases. Each edition retains its own source entry point; listing a source does not establish that its current content or a specific subsection has been read.

Washington-specific WAC provisions, WCCC/MERIT processes, DCYF procedures, numerical staffing/training/drill rules and reporting deadlines were not transferred into the other states' answer bodies. The books do not carry the DOCX's historical checked dates as current checks. Scenario facts do not establish legal thresholds.

This is an editorial writing and presentation task. Current primary-rule verification, precise subsection mapping and independent regulatory/clinical/bilingual review were not completed as part of it. The new records retain `current_rule_verified: false` and `checked_date: null`. No reviewer identities, agency approvals, training credits, certification, official exam pass marks or current-law assurances were created.

The “Required actions (draft)” section should not be mistaken for a complete list of legal duties. Binding requirements depend on the actual license category, relevant ages and roles, local requirements, individual plans, and program participation. Cases that use household facts or a center-specific role retain an applicability caution.

The existing exact-subsection coverage gap is not resolved merely by changing the narrative. A future source-based revision must identify the effective primary text, clause, conditions, exceptions, recipients, deadlines and supporting records separately for each jurisdiction, then review both language versions.

## Opening the books

Download `48_State_Daily_Training_Style_Books.zip`, extract it with its folders intact, then open `START_HERE.html` in a browser. Individual books are in `books/` under their original filenames. Reading and controls need no account or internet connection; official-source links require network access.

GitHub normally shows HTML source rather than rendering an interactive local book. Use the ZIP for the working reader and its linked pages. No localhost preview links are provided.
