1. A calm relationship comes first

先建立平静、可靠的关系

Goal: Respond to distress with a safe limit and calm support.

儿童不安时，用平静支持和具体安全界限回应。

Move close enough to help safely. Notice what the child is communicating. Use a short sentence, then offer a manageable next step. Keep the response suited to the child's age and communication needs.

在安全距离内靠近帮助，留意儿童想表达什么。用一句简短话回应，再提供可做到的下一步，并考虑年龄和沟通需要。

Everyday example

A preschooler cries at arrival. Say, “You miss Dad. I will stay with you. Would you like to sit by the books or hold your bag?”

学前儿童入园时哭了。可以说：“你想爸爸了。我陪着你。你想坐在图书旁，还是先拿着书包？”

Practice

Role-play a calm response to a child who does not want to stop playing. Include one safe limit and one realistic choice.

Suggested feedback

A useful response acknowledges the child's feeling, gives a clear safe limit, and offers an achievable choice. Do not shame the child or offer a choice you cannot honor.

Sources still to verify: NAEYC_DAP

2. Observe before making a judgment

先观察，不急着下结论

Goal: Write an observation without diagnosing or labeling a child.

写出观察事实，不给儿童贴标签或作诊断。

Record what you saw and heard, the setting, and the adult response. Look for patterns over time and talk with the family respectfully. A milestone resource can guide questions but does not turn the educator into a clinician.

记录看到、听到的事实、环境和成人回应，留意一段时间内的模式，并尊重地与家庭交流。发育资料可以帮助提出问题，但不能让教师代替医疗人员诊断。

Everyday example

Write, “During three group songs, Kai watched the gestures and joined one movement,” rather than “Kai is behind.”

写“在三首集体歌曲中，Kai看着动作，并跟做了一个动作”，而不是“Kai发育落后”。

Practice

Rewrite “Lena never listens” using a fictional, observable event. Identify what information is still missing.

Suggested feedback

State the actual words or actions and context. Avoid “always” or “never” unless the evidence truly supports it. If concerns persist, follow the applicable family communication and referral pathway; do not invent a diagnosis.

Sources still to verify: CDC_MILESTONES, IDEA, NAEYC_DAP

3. Use play to support learning

在游戏中支持学习

Goal: Extend a child's play without taking it over.

帮助儿童延伸游戏，而不完全替他们决定。

Provide developmentally appropriate materials that are safe for the children using them. Watch the child's idea, describe what you notice, and offer a small prompt. Keep safety and supervision in place.

提供适合儿童发展且安全的材料，先看儿童在想什么，再描述观察到的现象，给一个小提示。始终保持安全看护。

Everyday example

A preschooler builds a bridge with suitable blocks. Ask, “What could make the bridge steady?” rather than rebuilding it for the child.

学前儿童用合适积木搭桥，可以问：“怎样让桥更稳？”而不是直接替儿童重新搭好。

Practice

Name one learning goal for block play and one question that lets the child try an idea.

Suggested feedback

A possible goal is exploring balance or explaining a plan. The question should invite thinking and leave room for the child's attempt. Check materials and individual needs rather than assume the example suits infants.

Sources still to verify: NAEYC_DAP

4. Talk, listen and share books

说、听和一起阅读

Goal: Create a short back-and-forth conversation.

创造简短的来回交流。

Follow the child's interest and leave time for a response, including gestures or other communication methods. Describe familiar activities and share age-appropriate books. Respect the family's languages and ways of communicating.

跟随儿童兴趣，留时间让其回应，包括手势或其他沟通方式。描述熟悉活动，一起读合适图书，尊重家庭语言和沟通方式。

Everyday example

A child points to a dog in a book. Say, “You found the dog. It is running.” Pause so the child can respond.

儿童指着书里的狗，可以说：“你找到小狗了。它在跑。”然后停一停，让儿童回应。

Practice

Write a three-turn exchange about a familiar picture without turning every sentence into a test question.

Suggested feedback

Include an adult comment, a possible child response, and an adult follow-up connected to the child's meaning. Accept different communication methods and do not demand a spoken answer from every child.

Sources still to verify: NAEYC_DAP, CDC_MILESTONES

5. Find early math in daily routines

在日常生活中发现早期数学

Goal: Use a familiar routine to explore quantity, size or pattern.

用熟悉日常活动探索数量、大小或规律。

Use safe, age-appropriate objects and a meaningful routine. Compare, sort or notice patterns with the child. Keep the experience playful and flexible rather than requiring a worksheet as proof of learning.

使用安全、适龄物品，在有意义的日常活动中比较、分类或寻找规律。保持游戏性和灵活性，不把做练习纸当成学习的唯一证明。

Everyday example

At a pretend preschool picnic, ask whether there are enough large play plates for the toy guests. Let children match one plate to each guest.

学前假装野餐时，看看给玩偶客人准备的大号游戏盘够不够，让儿童给每位客人对应一个盘子。

Practice

Plan a safe sorting activity for a specified age group. Explain how a child with different access needs can join.

Suggested feedback

Name the age group, safe materials, a simple goal and an accessible way to participate. Do not use small objects where they pose a choking risk.

Sources still to verify: NAEYC_DAP

6. Keep the goal, adapt the way to join

保留目标，调整参与方式

Goal: Suggest an individual support for a meaningful activity.

为有意义的活动提出适合个人的支持。

Ask what the child needs to participate and involve the family in understanding useful supports. Consider the environment, materials, timing and communication. Assess the particular request; do not assume one method must fit every child.

了解儿童需要什么才能参与，与家庭讨论有用支持，考虑环境、材料、时间和沟通，不假设每个儿童都必须用同一种方式。

Everyday example

For painting, a wider handle, a stable work surface or another accessible tool may let a child explore the same colors alongside peers.

绘画时，较粗手柄、稳定桌面或其他可用工具，可能让儿童与同伴一起探索相同颜色。

Practice

For a fictional child, propose two ways to participate in an activity while preserving its learning purpose.

Suggested feedback

Connect each support to the stated access need. Legal obligations require separate review of the program and applicable rules; do not promise or reject every request automatically.

Sources still to verify: ADA_CHILDCARE, IDEA, NAEYC_DAP

7. Make family communication usable

让家园沟通真正可用

Goal: Explain a practical plan in words the family can use.

用家庭能理解和使用的话解释实际安排。

Use short sentences and the family's appropriate communication method. Explain the action, the person responsible and what happens next. Check understanding kindly and improve unclear instructions.

用短句和合适沟通方式，说明做什么、谁负责、下一步是什么。友善确认理解，发现不清楚就改进说明。

Everyday example

Instead of “follow the escalation procedure,” explain which listed contact to try next if the first pickup contact cannot be reached.

不要只说“按升级流程”，而要说明第一个接送联系人联系不上时，下一位应联系谁。

Practice

Rewrite a jargon-heavy pickup message and invite the family to explain the plan in their own words.

Suggested feedback

A clear message names the specific next step and contact. Teach-back checks whether the explanation worked; it should not embarrass the family.

Sources still to verify: NAEYC_DAP, ADA_CHILDCARE

8. Know what a practice answer can and cannot prove

明白练习答案能证明什么

Goal: Separate a general safe action from an unresolved legal question.

区分一般安全行动和未解决的法律问题。

A workbook can help you rehearse a response. It does not replace current rules, emergency plans, qualified medical advice or demonstrated skill. When a legal answer needs a rule that has not been supplied, identify the missing information rather than guess.

练习册可帮助演练，但不能代替现行规则、应急计划、专业医疗建议或实际技能展示。法律题缺少适用规则时，应指出缺什么，不猜答案。

Everyday example

You can explain why an unverified adult should not take over supervision without inventing a nationwide staffing ratio.

你可以解释为什么不能让资格未核实的人接手看护，而不编造全国统一人员比例。

Practice

For one book exercise, identify a general action, one fact that affects legal applicability, and the official text still needed.

Suggested feedback

Keep the general action and the legal research distinct. A source entry point or a correct practice response alone does not prove legal compliance or earn approved training credit.

Sources still to verify: CCDF, HEADSTART
