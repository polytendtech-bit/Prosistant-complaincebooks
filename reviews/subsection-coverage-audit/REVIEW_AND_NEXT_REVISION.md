# Washington and California: subsection coverage audit

October 15, 2026 (Pacific time)

## Finding

The user's criticism is supported by the audit. The preceding revision changed narrative presentation while preserving the same underlying situation/task structure. It did not create distinct subsection-based regulatory coverage. The earlier claim that the new format was a rewrite must be read as a style rewrite, not as a requirements-based curriculum revision.

Both uploaded files are identical to the latest corresponding repository books. Every one of the 1,000 corresponding scenarios, questions, required-action answers, practical answers and records/good-practice answers is identical between Washington and California. The jurisdiction-specific differences are primarily the source leads, state labels and verification text.

These counts come from the actual uploaded HTML, matched to the retained content data. The zero mapping-field result does not mean no rule section links are displayed: 27 unique reference links appear in Washington cards and seven in California cards. A section link or manual entry point does not identify the operative clause, condition or exception a case is meant to teach.

Some similar scenarios can legitimately teach different requirements. Similarity alone does not establish a duplicate. A new requirement-specific learning objective and supported legal analysis are needed before counting the similar scenario as additional coverage.

## What changes in the next revision

The unit of authoring becomes **one distinct requirement**, not one scenario multiplied by ten decision prompts. Existing IDs remain in the tracking register, but their allocation must be reconsidered after the actual requirements are extracted.

For every candidate case, complete the following before treating it as state-specific curriculum:

1. State and local jurisdiction; license or registration category; ages; relevant program participation.
2. Issuing authority, authoritative source URL, retained text, retrieval date and effective period.
3. Exact section and subsection/paragraph locator, including any cross-reference.
4. One primary requirement stated plainly: who must do what, under which facts and conditions.
5. Applicable exceptions, thresholds, notices, recipients, timing and records. Unknowns stay unknown.
6. A different factual problem from other cases, or a clearly different condition/exception that changes the answer.
7. A Chinese/English narrative that contains the facts needed to decide the issue.
8. A question about that particular requirement, not a generic pressure, helper, privacy or inspection prompt attached to every topic.
9. An answer separating the verified obligation from practical suggestions, with the actual supporting citation.
10. Independent legal/regulatory and bilingual review, recorded truthfully.

One case may use secondary requirements, but it needs a designated primary target. The same case must not be counted as several distinct exercises merely because it is filed under several headings. A general question such as “what if you work alone?” becomes a separate regulatory case only when it exercises a different supported condition or duty.

Washington and California need separate source maps. If the same facts are useful in both, each answer must be grounded independently in the applicable state's requirements. Identical general safety reasoning is not proof that the legal duties, exceptions and reporting routes are identical.

## Examples of distinct authoring targets

These are **candidate learning targets**, not claims that a particular unpublished subsection contains these exact duties. The operative text still needs to be read and mapped.

Within a supervision topic, distinguish:

- An adult cannot see a play area because furniture blocks the view: the case tests positioning and visibility.
- Two adults disagree about who accepted care during a handoff: the case tests transfer of responsibility.
- An attendance total matches but a particular child is unaccounted for: the case tests child-by-child reconciliation and, if confirmed missing, emergency response.
- An adult is present but not eligible to perform the assigned role: the case tests role qualification and staffing, not merely adult visibility.

The proposed short cases below illustrate a different factual focus. They are not approved California or Washington answers and have no invented subsection numbers.

### Candidate A: a blocked view / 看护视线受阻

**Situation:** During quiet play, a tall storage shelf blocks the caregiver's view of the reading corner. All children are on the attendance list and no handoff is taking place. A worker says that being in the same room is enough.

**情境：** 安静活动时，一个高柜挡住了照护者看向阅读角的视线。名单上的孩子都在，没有发生交接。员工认为只要人在同一个房间就够了。

**Question:** What change would restore the needed observation and assistance while the applicable supervision provision is checked?

**问题：** 在核对看护条款时，怎样调整位置或布局，让成人能够完成必要的观察和帮助？

**Draft response:** Reposition the adult or move the activity to a safely supervised area, and correct the layout before relying on that corner again. A count alone does not resolve a blocked view. The exact required mode of supervision must come from the rule for the actual setting and age group.

**初稿处理：** 调整成人位置，或把活动移到能安全照看的区域；解决布局问题后再依赖原区域。人数正确不能解决视线被挡。具体需要怎样观察，要按实际场所和年龄对应的条款确认。

### Candidate B: no clear handoff / 交接责任未明确

**Situation:** Two qualified adults can see the doorway, but each believes the other has accepted a newly arrived child. The child remains with the sending adult, and the receiving list has not been updated.

**情境：** 两名合格成人都看得见门口，却各以为对方已经接手新到的孩子。孩子仍在原照护者身边，接收名单尚未更新。

**Question:** What must the adults establish before the sending adult leaves?

**问题：** 原照护者离开前，两人必须说清并核对什么？

**Draft response:** Name the child, obtain clear acceptance and reconcile the receiving list. Keep responsibility with the sending adult until the handoff is complete. This case targets responsibility transfer rather than an obstructed view; the legal mapping remains pending.

**初稿处理：** 说清孩子姓名，等接手成人明确回应，再核对接收名单。交接完成前，原照护者继续负责。本题针对责任转移，与视线受阻的目标不同；法律条款仍待映射。

These examples show the intended change in case design. They do not replace the missing source research or establish a completed book revision.
