100 foundational teaching prototypes

Provisional editorial draft. Current authoritative sources could not be accessed. These examples are not verified state-law answers, an approved course, medical instructions, or a validated examination. Respond to real emergencies through the appropriate emergency plan immediately.

One prototype per original situation family. This condenses repeated material; it does not replace the pending state/category legal review. Original incident wording is retained. The new action notes are plain-language authoring examples.

FAM-001 · Doorway handoff

Supervision & transitions · Original range CORE-0001–CORE-0010

Situation

A child moves through a connecting doorway while both adults believe the other is supervising.

Try before reading

For this situation, what is the immediate concern and a practical next action? Explain how children remain safely supervised.

Provisional teaching note

Say, “Maya, you have Leo now.” Maya replies, “I have Leo.” Both check Leo against the attendance list.

Authoring correction

Name the adult who accepts care, and require a reply before the first adult leaves.

Local facts still needed

Supervision duties; transition records; missing-child response; exact center/home license rules.

Unverified source leads: HEADSTART_SUPERVISION; CCDF
